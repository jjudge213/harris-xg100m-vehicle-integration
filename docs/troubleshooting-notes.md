# Troubleshooting Notes

Private draft. Add real notes only after sanitization.

Use this format for field-service style documentation:

## Case Template

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Symptom | What was observed? |
| Context | What changed before the issue appeared? |
| Inspection | What was checked visually or mechanically? |
| Measurement | What was measured or tested? |
| Diagnosis | What was the likely cause? |
| Corrective Action | What was changed? |
| Verification | How was the fix confirmed? |

## Candidate Cases To Document

### Speaker Audio Check

- Symptom: weak, muffled, distorted, or rattling audio after panel installation.
- Investigation: grille clearance, fastener tension, speaker obstruction, loose panel, wiring strain.
- Verification: non-sensitive audio source, repeatable volume check, no rattle after road/vibration check.

### Cable Strain / Intermittent Audio

- Symptom: audio cuts out or changes when console/panel moves.
- Investigation: connector seating, wire route, bend radius, strain relief, pinch points.
- Verification: continuity check and movement test.

### RF / Feedline Check

- Symptom: poor transmit/receive performance or high reflected power.
- Investigation: antenna mount, feedline route, connector condition, grounding/bonding.
- Verification: sanitized measurement summary only; do not publish operational frequencies.
