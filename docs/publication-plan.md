## Current Recommendation

Keep this repository private until it has at least one of the following:

- safe photos or recreated diagrams for radio body/control-head placement
- sanitized power/fusing/grounding notes
- antenna/feedline routing notes
- a short verification checklist with non-sensitive results

The current speaker integration evidence is good, but by itself it is not enough to present as a complete XG-100M vehicle installation.

## Public-Ready Criteria

- README clearly scopes what is complete and what is planned.
- Every image reviewed for sensitive data.
- No operational radio configuration appears anywhere.
- No proprietary Harris material is included.
- Diagrams use placeholders and public-safe labels.
- Claims are limited to what the evidence supports.
